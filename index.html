Executive AI Roadmap

exec-roadmap.ai — a service of Applied Agents LLC

What this is

Your personal AI 30/60/90-day roadmap. Built for you specifically — your industry, your role, your AI and technical level, your goals, your resources. Whether you are starting from zero or already using these tools daily, the roadmap tells you what to do in the next ninety days for maximum return on your time, effort, and money.

It is not a course, not a subscription, and not a tool you have to learn. It is a document, built for you, after a conversation.

Who this is for

Executives who need to get good at AI and do not have an obvious way to do it.

Specifically:

  • You are senior enough that nobody is going to hand you a training plan
  • You have tried some of this already, or you have not, and either way you are not sure whether what you are doing is the right thing to be doing
  • You do not have time to sort through a thousand courses, newsletters, tools, and opinions to find the ten percent that matter for someone in your position
  • Your firm is probably too small to have an internal AI function you can lean on

You do not need to be technical. You do not need to have a plan. You do not need to know what you want.

The problem this solves

There is no shortage of AI material. There is a shortage of AI material that is right for you — your role, your industry, your level, your time budget.

So most executives do one of three things. They read everything and retain nothing. They take a general course built for a general audience and it does not connect to their actual work. Or they do nothing and hope it resolves itself.

All three waste the scarcest thing you have, which is your time. The roadmap exists to spend that time well.

What you get

A written 30/60/90-day roadmap, built for your context.

The roadmap is specific and actionable. Depending on your goals and where you are starting from, it will include things like:

  • Training to take — the specific book, course, or program that fits your level and your goal. Free or paid, whichever is the better use of your time and money. Not a reading list of forty items; the two or three that are right for you.
  • Tools to try — which ones, in what order, for what purpose, and how to get started with each. Claude Desktop, Claude Code, and whatever else fits your work.
  • What to do with your own week — where to apply this to work already on your desk, so the learning is not separate from the job.
  • What to skip — the things that are getting attention right now and are not worth yours.
  • How to tell if it is working — what should be different at day 30, day 60, day 90.

Everything is sequenced. First things first, in the order that gets you the most for the least.

Questions people ask before we talk

How much of my time does this take?

About two hours total: one context conversation, one delivery session. The roadmap itself is my work, not yours.

I already use ChatGPT every day. Is this still for me?

Yes, and the roadmap will look different. For someone already fluent, the question is not "how do I start" but "what is the next level, and what am I missing." That is what the context conversation establishes.

I know almost nothing. Is that a problem?

No. Starting from zero is common and the roadmap is built for where you actually are, not where you feel you should be.

Is this confidential?

Yes. Nothing about you, your firm, or our conversation goes anywhere. Part of the value here is having a place to ask beginner questions without an audience.

Is this specific to my industry?

The roadmap is built on your context, so yes. My deepest experience is in capital markets and fixed income, and financial services generally. If you are outside that, say so on the call and I will tell you honestly whether I am the right person.

What does the roadmap actually look like?

A written document, typically five to ten pages, organized by what to do in the first 30 days, the next 30, and the final 30. Specific recommendations with reasons attached, not general advice.

Are you going to try to sell me something afterward?

No. The hourly work exists if you want it. If you do not, the engagement is finished when the roadmap is delivered.

What if it is not useful?

Tell me on the delivery call and I will rework it. The roadmap is only worth something if you actually use it.

Who else have you done this for?

I have done this work informally with several executives and general managers. This is a newly productized service, so I do not yet have public case studies, and I would rather say that plainly than dress it up. The upside for early clients is my full attention.
